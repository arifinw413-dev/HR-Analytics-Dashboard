# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI Project

![HR Analytics Dashboard](HR%20ANALYST%20DASHBOARD.jpg)

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project analyzes Human Resources (HR) data to understand employee retention patterns, compensation structures, department distribution, and the key drivers behind employee attrition (turnover). 

With this interactive dashboard, Management and HR teams can make data-driven decisions to boost employee retention and optimize workforce allocation.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI (Power Query, DAX, Data Modeling)
* **Data Source:** Raw CSV Data (`HR_Analytics-raw&clean.csv`)
* **Design & Layout:** Custom UI/UX Dashboard Layout

---

## 📊 Key Features & Analysis Highlights

### 1. Demographics & Employee Structure
* **Total Headcount:** Track active vs. inactive employee counts.
* **Departmental Breakdown:** Workforce distribution across departments and job roles.
* **Demographics:** Employee segmentation by age group, gender, and education level.

### 2. Attrition & Retention Analysis
* **Attrition Rate:** Overall employee turnover percentage[cite: 1].
* **Key Attrition Drivers:** Relationship between attrition and job satisfaction, work-life balance, commute distance, and tenure[cite: 1].

### 3. Compensation & Performance Insight
* **Salary Distribution:** Average salary patterns by role and experience level[cite: 1].
* **Years at Company vs. Promotion:** Evaluation of career progression and employee promotions[cite: 1].
